## Brand & Style

The design system powers an in-store ordering web application tailored for a lively Indonesian fresh milk, snack, and dine-in eatery. The interface caters directly to seated dine-in patrons scanning QR codes on their tables. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Segar & Menyenangkan (Fresh & Delightful):** Visual cues evoke cold, creamy fresh milk, hot crisp snacks, and communal warmth.
- **Efisiensi Cepat (Swift & Direct):** Because customers are dining on premise, the experience eliminates unnecessary e-commerce friction (no delivery addresses, shipping tiers, or courier options).
- **Modern Nusantara Warmth:** Balancing clean, structured layouts with friendly, soft forms and inviting color accents.

### Design Aesthetic: Tactile-Friendly Modern F&B
The system merges clean modern corporate precision with soft, tactile hospitality elements. It features generous pill radii, airy pale-blue surfaces, high-contrast typography, and tactile active states that mimic physical touchpoints on mobile screens.

## Colors

The palette establishes an appetizing, dairy-fresh atmosphere grounded by deep corporate navy for legible menus and energetic red accents for promotional triggers and order calls-to-action.

### Roles & Semantic Distribution
- **Primary Blue (`#0052CC`):** The core brand anchor. Used for primary navigation states, active category tabs, interactive toggles, order status cards, and primary buttons.
- **Bright Red Accent (`#FF3838`):** The sensory appetite stimulus. Reserved for high-priority purchase actions (e.g., "Pesan Sekarang", "+ Tambah"), item modifier alerts, promotional badges ("Best Seller", "Favorit"), and critical notices.
- **Surface & Foundation Tones:**
  - Base Background: `#F4F8FF` (Soft surface blue that prevents screen glare in ambient indoor lighting).
  - Surface Highlight: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for food cards, modal dialogs, and sticky footers).
  - Interactive Container Tint: `#E6F0FF` (Light milk-tint blue for unselected chips, table badges, and counter steppers).
- **Typography & Structural Neutrals:**
  - Dark Navy (`#172554`): Primary heading and high-emphasis body text color. Replaces harsh pitch black with a rich, dairy-blue undertone.
  - Muted Blue-Gray (`#687280`): Secondary descriptors, category counts, and ingredient notes.
  - Border Subtle (`#D0E2FF`): Clean structural dividing lines, card borders, and input strokes.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all interfaces. Its geometric balance, open counters, and humanist curves deliver exceptional legibility on handheld devices while conveying a playful, energetic presence.

### Indonesian Currency Standard
All monetary figures strictly follow formal Indonesian rupiah conventions:
- Standard: Prefix `Rp` with no trailing space or period, using dot thousand-separators (e.g., `Rp10.000`, `Rp18.500`, `Rp120.000`).
- Micro-copy or compact price pills preserve the `Rp` prefix to maintain instantaneous cost comprehension during rapid table ordering.

### Hierarchy & Role Distribution
- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tighter letter-spacing and firm font-weights (`600`–`800`) to stand out against rich food photography.
- **Body & Captions:** Tuned for high contrast in ambient restaurant lighting with `#172554` for primary reading and `#687280` for secondary flavor descriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

The web app is optimized primarily for single-hand mobile interactions (viewports `360px` to `430px`), while fluidly adapting for tablet terminals (`768px` to `1024px`) used at counter stations or shared table displays.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Mobile First (Fluid Column Grid):** In-store guests access the app through smartphones. The menu renders as a responsive 1-column list or 2-column card grid with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `0.75rem` gutters.
- **Sticky Viewport Anchors:** The header contains persistent table metadata (`Meja 08`), search, and call-waiter triggers. The bottom canvas reserves an unyielding sticky checkout strip (`space-xl` height clearance) to summarize the active cart.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (2-column item grid, sticky single-action bottom bar).
  - Tablet: `640px`–`1024px` (3-column grid with split-pane order drawer on the right).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is kept light, luminous, and fluid—mirroring fresh milk rather than heavy layered drop-shadows.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** Soft surface blue (`#F4F8FF`). Sets an ambient foundation.
2. **Level 1 (Cards & Lists):** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle border (`1px solid #D0E2FF`) and an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 82, 204, 0.05)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Tabs, Popovers, & Quantity Steppers):** Elevated elements use `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 82, 204, 0.10)`.
4. **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Bar & Modal Sheets):** High tactile presence. Uses `0 -8px 28px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.08)` to clearly separate checkout controls from scrollable product lists.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape language, bringing a soft, organic warmth to cards, sheets, and touch targets.

- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem):** Input text fields, table status indicators, and quantity stepper controls.
- **Product Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Menu item containers, dine-in confirmation dialogs, and selection sheets.
- **Pills & Floating Action Elements (`rounded-full`):** Category filter chips (`Minuman`, `Snack`, `Makanan Berat`), status badges, and checkout CTAs.

## Components

### Table Number Dine-In Badge
- **Style:** Compact pill fixed in the top navigation.
- **Visuals:** `#E6F0FF` background, `#0052CC` border and bold text (`Meja 08 - Dine-In`).
- **Behavior:** Persistent across all views to confirm table association without requiring customer re-entry.

### Buttons & Quick Actions
- **Primary Order Button ("Pesan Sekarang" / "+ Tambah"):** Background `#FF3838`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill shape with bold font weight. Press state slightly deepens to `#E02424`.
- **Secondary Action ("Ubah Opsi", "Panggil Pelayan"):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #0052CC`, text `#0052CC`.
- **Quantity Steppers (- 1 +):** Pill container in `#E6F0FF`, icon buttons in `#0052CC`, centered counter text in `#172554`.

### Menu Category Tabs (Chips)
- **Categories:** `Minuman (Fresh Milk)`, `Snack`, and `Makanan Berat`.
- **Inactive State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D0E2FF`, text `#687280`.
- **Active State:** Background `#0052CC`, border `1px solid #0052CC`, text `#FFFFFF`, soft glow shadow.
- **Behavior:** Horizontally scrollable row with sticky snapping beneath the top header.

### Food & Drink Cards
- **Orientation:** Dual format (vertical 2-column cards for quick browsing; horizontal single-column cards inside category listings).
- **Structure:** Rounded container (`1rem`), 1:1 appetizing image thumbnail, item name in `#172554`, flavor/size notes in `#687280`, Indonesian price formatting (`Rp12.000`) in `#0052CC`, and a quick-add trigger button in `#FF3838`.

### Item Customization Modal (Milk Modifiers)
- **Surface:** Bottom sheet with top rounded corners (`1.5rem`).
- **Options:** Sugar level (Normal, Kurang Manis, Tanpa Gula), Ice level (Es Normal, Sedikit Es, Hangat), and Toppings (+ Rp3.000 Cincau / Jelly).
- **Selection Control:** Radio-chips with `#0052CC` selected rings and checkmarks.

### Sticky Order Summary Bar
- **Position:** Docked to bottom viewport safe-area.
- **Layout:** Flex container showing total item count and accumulated total (`Rp48.000`), paired with a high-contrast `#FF3838` button labeled "Lihat Pesanan".
